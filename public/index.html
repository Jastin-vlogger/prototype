// OUR STORY //

The Journey

In 1998, Chef Fatima Al-Rashid opened a small kitchen in Dubai Marina with a dream — to share her mother's recipes with the world. With nothing but a passion for home-cooked meals and an unwavering commitment to quality, The Mother Restaurant was born.

The name "The Mother" comes from the belief that a mother's love is the secret ingredient in every great meal — a philosophy that has guided us for over two decades.

From Humble Beginnings

Our first location was a modest 40-seat bistro. Word of mouth spread quickly about the exceptional food and warm hospitality. Within two years, we had outgrown our space and moved to our current location — a stunning 200-seat restaurant with panoramic views of the Arabian Gulf.

The Pursuit of Perfection

Every dish at The Mother Restaurant is a testament to our commitment to love and quality. We source ingredients from the UAE's finest local farms and the world's most prestigious producers.

Looking Forward

As we look to the future, we remain committed to our founding principles: love in every detail, respect for ingredients, and creating unforgettable experiences for our guests. The next chapter of The Mother Restaurant is just beginning.

The Art of Perfect Wagyu

F
Chef Fatima Al-Rashid
Executive Chef

Wagyu beef is widely regarded as the finest beef in the world. Originating from Japan, the term "wagyu" literally means "Japanese cow" and refers to several breeds of cattle known for their intense marbling and rich flavor.

Understanding the Grades

Japanese Wagyu is graded on a scale from A1 to A5, with A5 being the highest quality. The grading considers marbling, color, brightness, firmness, and texture. At The Mother Restaurant, we exclusively serve A5 Wagyu from the renowned Miyazaki prefecture.

Cooking Techniques

The key to cooking Wagyu is simplicity. The beef's natural flavor and texture should be the star. We recommend minimal seasoning — just sea salt and freshly ground black pepper — and cooking over high heat for a perfect sear.
